# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (14 October 2026)

First release.

- Five shifts, eleven policy rules, three ticket types: access requests, agent scope
  requests and recertifications.
- AutoReview, an AI pre-screener that is wrong in documented places, and a final audit
  report that sets its claimed 97% against what it did on your desk and measures your
  automation bias.
- Every week is a new seeded bank. The generator and the validator are independent, and
  13 tests check that they agree on 22,000 generated tickets.
- Light and dark themes, phone layout, keyboard play, reduced motion, screen-reader
  announcements, optional synthesised sound, relaxed mode without a clock.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages under a strict Content-Security-Policy, and as a single offline
  HTML file.
